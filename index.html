ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding one public space through real local data.

This Community Engagement Project studies how a public space is used by different groups, digitally maps its facilities and documents usage patterns and improvement requirements.

The selected site is Diamond Garden (Late Shri N. G. Acharya Udyan) in Chembur. MCGM lists the garden at the junction of MDS Marg and V. N. Purav Marg with an area of 9,798 sq. m.

Online research is used only for background information. The website now includes photographs from the field visit. Exact visitor counts and survey percentages should be added only after the observation/survey is completed.

ISSUES & IMPROVEMENTS

Observed issues and suggested improvements.

The following issues were identified during the field visit and can be considered for improvement.

01

Cracked benches

Some benches in the garden are cracked or damaged, which can reduce seating comfort and may create a safety concern for visitors. Damaged benches should be repaired or replaced to provide safe and comfortable seating.

Observed
02

Spitting near dustbins

Spitting was observed near the dustbin area, affecting cleanliness and the overall hygiene of the public space. Clear cleanliness signage, awareness measures and regular monitoring near dustbins can help reduce this problem.
